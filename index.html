Privacy Policy

Inventory Stock Manager App — Last updated: August 21, 2026

Inventory Stock Manager App ("the app") is an offline stock/inventory tracker: you add products, sizes, and stock quantities, and everything you enter stays on your own device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server operated by us — the app has nowhere to send your data even if it wanted to. This page explains what data is, and isn't, collected when you use it.

Information We Collect

The app itself does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server — it has no backend and no user accounts. We do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or health data, and the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of any kind.

Information Stored Only on Your Device

Everything you enter — product names, sizes, stock quantities, sale records, product photos, and your selected app language — is saved locally on your device using standard on-device storage (a local database and local file storage). None of it is uploaded, synced, or sent anywhere. If you delete a product or uninstall the app, that data is gone, because it never existed anywhere else.

Camera & Photo Library Access

If you choose to attach a photo to a product, the app asks for camera or photo library access to let you pick or take that photo. The photo is resized and saved into the app's private local storage on your device only — it is never uploaded to us or to any third party.

Subscriptions & Purchases

The app offers an optional Pro upgrade. Purchases are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play, and subscription status is managed for us by RevenueCat, a third-party subscription platform. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated app user identifier and purchase/transaction data needed to validate your entitlement — we do not share your name, email, or payment details with them, because we never collect that information ourselves. See RevenueCat's own privacy policy for details on their data handling:

  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Advertising

Free-tier use of the app displays ads served by Google AdMob on both iOS and Android, to support the app being free. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect information such as your advertising identifier, IP address, device information, and ad interaction data. This app does not request the App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS, so per Google's documented behavior, ads shown are non-personalized/contextual rather than ads personalized to your activity across other apps or websites. This data collection and use is governed by Google's own policies, not by us directly:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Depending on your region, you may have choices around ad personalization through your device's ad settings (e.g. Android Settings → Ads, or iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us so we can address it.

Data Security

Because the app has no backend and transmits no personal data of its own, there is no app-managed remote storage of your information to secure. Data stored locally on your device is protected by your device's own operating system security. Any data handling associated with ads or subscriptions is performed by Google or RevenueCat under their own security and privacy practices.

Your Choices

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes everything it stored on your device (products, sizes, stock, sales history, and photos), since none of it exists anywhere else. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time from your Apple ID or Google Play account settings, or via "Manage Subscription" in the app.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a new feature or SDK is added — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
