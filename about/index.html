Everything you need to track stock

Built for small shops, market sellers, online resellers and anyone who keeps stock and wants a quick, clear view of it.

  • 📦

    Products with photos

    Add a name, photo, price, SKU and description for every product, so you can recognise items at a glance.

  • 📏

    Stock by size

    Give each product as many sizes as it needs and set the quantity for each one with simple +/– steppers.

  • 🗂️

    Your own categories

    Create lists such as Color, Brand or Provider and attach them to products to keep a large catalog organised.

  • 🔎

    Search and stock filters

    Find any product instantly and filter by All, In Stock, Low Stock or Out of Stock to see what needs restocking.

  • 🛒

    Record sales in seconds

    Pick a product, size, quantity and date. Stock updates automatically when a sale is saved.

  • 📈

    Sales history

    Review every recorded sale with product, size, quantity and date, and search through past sales.

  • 🌍

    15 languages

    English, Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, French, German, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian, Russian, Romanian, Arabic, Turkish, Dutch and Indonesian.

  • 🔒

    Private and offline

    No account, no sign-in and no server. Everything you enter is stored locally on your device.

How it works

From an empty app to a tracked catalog in a few minutes.

  1. Add your products

    Take or choose a photo, enter a name, price and SKU, then add sizes with their starting quantities.

  2. Sell and record

    When something sells, record it with the product, size, quantity and date. Stock goes down automatically.

  3. Check what is low

    Filter by Low Stock or Out of Stock and see exactly what needs reordering.

Free to start, Pro if you want more

The app is free to download and use, supported by ads. Inventory Pro is an optional upgrade that removes ads and unlocks the sales history. Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID and can be cancelled at any time.
